# Security Policy

This policy applies to every repository under [github.com/docxology](https://github.com/docxology) that does not ship its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue** for security problems.

1. **Preferred:** use GitHub private vulnerability reporting — open the affected repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Reports stay private between you and the maintainer.
2. **Alternative:** email Daniel@ActiveInference.Institute (see also [security.txt](https://danielarifriedman.com/.well-known/security.txt)).

Please include the repository, affected commit or version, reproduction steps, and the impact you observed.

## What to expect

- Acknowledgement within 7 days.
- An assessment and, where a fix is warranted, a patch or mitigation plan. Coordinated disclosure timing will be agreed with you.
- Credit in the advisory if you want it.

## Scope

Most repositories here are research code, manuscripts, and teaching materials provided as-is. Archived repositories are read-only and do not receive fixes; please report issues in archived projects against their successor repository where one is named.
